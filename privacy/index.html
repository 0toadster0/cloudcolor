Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026 · Version 2 · Applies to Cloud Color for iPhone and iPad, version 1.0 and later

The short version

  • Your coloring stays on your device. Pictures, progress and settings are saved only on your iPhone or iPad.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. We don't use the advertising identifier and don't follow you across other apps or websites.
  • Usage statistics only if you say yes. When you first open the app, you choose Share and Begin or Don't Share. Statistics about how the app is used — like which pictures are started and finished — are sent only if you choose to share, and you can change your mind anytime.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your payment details. Our subscription service, RevenueCat, receives your purchase history and a random ID so the app can confirm Premium.
  • We never sell your information.

1. Who we are

Cloud Color (the "app") is made and operated by Hana Gurz ("we", "us"), who is responsible for the personal information described here (the "controller" under data protection laws such as the GDPR).

Contact: filterdudemain@gmail.com or the contact form.

2. Information that stays on your device

Your colored pictures and progress, your choices in the app (such as sounds, haptics and replays), hint counts, the picture the Home Screen widget shows, and a copy of your Premium status are stored only on your device. We can't see them. They are included in your device backups if you use iCloud or computer backups (handled by Apple under your own settings). Deleting the app deletes them from your device.

The app does not ask for, and cannot access, your name, email, contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera. When you share a finished picture, the app hands the image to the iOS share sheet; where it goes is your choice, and we receive nothing.

3. Usage statistics — only if you turn them on

When you first open the app, the welcome screen asks whether you'd like to share usage statistics: choose Share and Begin or Don't Share. No usage statistics are sent unless you choose to share. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Data (the gear on Home). The app works exactly the same either way.

If you choose Don't Share, the app sends no usage statistics and creates no analytics ID. Purchases still work as described in section 4: Apple and our subscription service, RevenueCat, still process the information needed to show prices, confirm and restore Premium.

What is shared if you turn it on

  • How the app is used, for example: which pictures are opened, started and finished (by the picture's name and catalog ID, category, difficulty and whether it is free, Starter, Daily or Premium), progress milestones (25%, 50%, 75%), whether a coloring is a first coloring or a recolor, hints, replays and the share button used, which screens are visited, taps on locked or Premium pictures, opening the app from its widget, whether the short tutorial was finished or skipped, how long coloring sessions last, Daily picture participation, when the app asks Apple to show its rating request, and the Premium screen: when it is shown, which plan is selected, and whether a purchase or restore succeeded, was cancelled or failed.
  • Technical context: app version and build, iOS version, iPhone or iPad, whether you have Premium, a random ID for the current app session, and the time of each event. For people who turned statistics on in their first session, also the day the app was installed and how many days ago that was.
  • A random analytics ID created on your device when you turn statistics on. It is not your name, email, Apple Account or advertising identifier. You can see and copy it in Settings → Privacy & Data.

What is never shared

Your name, email, Apple Account, contacts, photos, location, the advertising identifier, your artwork or anything you type. Activity from before you turned statistics on is never sent later. When you turn statistics off, sharing stops at once and anything not yet sent is deleted from your device.

Why

To understand and improve Cloud Color — for example which pictures people enjoy and finish, where the tutorial is confusing, whether people come back, and how the Premium screen works. We don't use statistics for advertising, don't sell them and don't combine them with information from other companies.

Who processes them

Statistics are processed for us by PostHog, Inc. (servers in the United States). PostHog keeps them under your random analytics ID; we never add your name or other personal details. The app asks PostHog not to work out your location from your IP address, and our PostHog project is set to discard IP addresses, so they are not stored with the statistics. Apart from PostHog, the analytics ID is not shared with anyone — including RevenueCat.

Legal basis (where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies): your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

4. Purchases and Premium

Premium is an optional auto-renewable subscription. You buy it with your Apple Account through Apple's in-app purchase system; Apple processes the payment, and we never see your payment details.

To show Premium prices, confirm and restore your Premium, and keep it working, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) as our subscription service provider. When the app connects to RevenueCat (for example when it opens, shows the Premium screen, or you buy or restore), RevenueCat receives:

  • a random customer ID created for the app on your device — not your name, email or Apple Account;
  • your App Store purchase history for Cloud Color (which products, purchase and renewal dates, subscription status and transaction records), if you have bought or restored anything;
  • basic technical information needed to validate purchases, such as the app version, iOS version, device type, and your App Store country, language and currency. Like any internet service, RevenueCat's servers also receive your IP address when the app connects.

RevenueCat processes this information on our behalf to provide these subscription services, including the purchase statistics we see in its dashboard (for example how many subscriptions were started or renewed). It does not use it to track you across apps. If you never buy Premium, RevenueCat holds only the random ID and technical information, with no purchase history.

Legal basis (where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies): performing our contract with you (providing Premium) and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and running the subscription.

5. When you contact us

If you write to us through the contact form or by email, we receive your email address, your message and anything you choose to include (such as your device model or app version). Messages sent with the form are delivered to our inbox by Formspree, Inc. (United States). We use this only to answer you and to look into what you report.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering your request.

6. This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may record visitors' IP addresses in server logs for security and reliability, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The contact form sends your message to Formspree only when you press Send message.

7. Information from Apple

Apple provides developers with summary download and sales figures, ratings and reviews you choose to publish, and — only if you allow it in your device settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers) — crash reports and aggregated usage information. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy and doesn't identify you to us.

8. What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • No account, sign-in, location, contacts or photo access.

10. International transfers

Our service providers (PostHog, RevenueCat, Formspree, GitHub) process information in the United States and possibly other countries. Where data protection laws require it — for example for people in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland — these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and their UK equivalent) in the providers' data processing terms, or on an adequacy decision where one applies.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Turn usage statistics on or off anytime in Settings → Privacy & Data. Turning them off withdraws your consent for the future; it doesn't affect statistics already shared.
  • Access or delete statistics already shared: contact us and include your analytics ID from Settings → Privacy & Data. Because we don't know who you are, the ID is how we find your data.
  • Delete what's on your device: delete the app.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel Premium in your Apple Account settings (see Support).
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold — for example your support messages, or the purchase records RevenueCat keeps for us — using the contact form or filterdudemain@gmail.com. Because the app has no accounts, we may ask for details that help us find the right records.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We will answer within one month, or sooner where your local law requires. We don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by California law, and we don't use it for targeted advertising or for decisions with legal effects.

12. Children

Cloud Color is made for a general audience. It is not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app has no accounts, chat or advertising, and usage statistics are only shared if someone chooses to share them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

Information leaves the app only over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and our providers protect it with industry-standard safeguards. No method is perfectly secure, but we keep what we collect to a minimum.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date and version above. For important changes we will tell you in the app and ask for your permission again where it is required.
