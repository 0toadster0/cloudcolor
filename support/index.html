How can we help?

Answers to common questions, help with Premium, and a way to reach us if you need a hand.

Common questions

Where are my colored pictures saved?

Only on your device — there's no account. Your progress saves as you color. Your in-progress and finished pictures are in My Art.

Will I keep my pictures on a new iPhone or iPad?

Yes, if you set up the new device from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one: the app's saved pictures come with the backup. If you delete the app without a backup, its pictures are deleted too, and we can't recover them.

How do I unlock more Starter Book pictures?

The first three Starter pictures are open right away. Color any one of them to 75% and the next picture unlocks; each different picture you take to 75% opens one more, up to picture 10.

The widget doesn't show my picture

Open the app once and color a little, then go back to the Home Screen; the widget refreshes within a few minutes (iOS decides exactly when). Removing and re-adding the widget can also help.

Does Cloud Color collect data about me?

Your coloring stays on your device. Usage statistics are shared only if you turn them on, and purchases are confirmed through Apple and our subscription service, RevenueCat. The details are in the Privacy Policy. To delete everything stored by the app on your device, delete the app.

How do I turn usage statistics on or off?

In the app, tap the gear on Home, then Privacy & Data → Share Usage Statistics. It's on only if you chose Share and Begin on the welcome screen or turned it on here, and the app works the same either way. To have statistics already shared deleted, send us your analytics ID (Settings → Privacy & Data → Copy Analytics ID) with the form below.

Premium, billing and subscriptions

Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold by Apple. Apple handles payment, renewals, cancellations and refunds — we can't see or change your payment details.

Cancel or change your subscription

  1. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Cloud Color, then choose a different plan or Cancel Subscription.

While you have Premium, Settings → Manage Subscription in the app takes you to the same place. Or open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions on your device. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge; Premium stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. Apple's guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

Restore Premium on a new device

Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought with, then in the app tap the gear on Home and choose Restore Purchases (it's also on the Premium screen).

Request a refund or report a billing problem

Refunds are decided by Apple: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Premium doesn't unlock after a purchase or restore, write to us below and choose Premium, billing or subscription.

Report a technical problem

Something not working? Use the form below and choose Report a problem. It helps a lot if you include:

  • your device and iOS version (for example "iPhone 15, iOS 26");
  • the app version — it's at the bottom of Settings in the app (the gear on Home);
  • the picture's name, what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

If a screenshot would help, mention it — we'll reply by email and you can attach it there.
