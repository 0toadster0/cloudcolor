Premium subscriptions

  • Premium is an optional auto-renewable subscription, offered monthly and yearly. The price and billing period are shown in the app before you buy.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings (how). Cancelling stops future renewals; Premium stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • If Premium ends, pictures that need Premium lock again; your progress on them stays on your device.
